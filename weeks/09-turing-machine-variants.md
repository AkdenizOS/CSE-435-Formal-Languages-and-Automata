# Week 9 — Variants of Turing Machines

> Syllabus (TR): *Turing makinası çeşitleri*

**Previous:** [Week 8](08-turing-machines.md) · **Next:** [Week 10](10-decidability.md)

## Goals
- Show that multitape, nondeterministic, and enumerator TMs are all equivalent to the basic model.
- State the Church-Turing thesis and explain what it is and is not.

## Key concepts
- **Multitape TM** ≡ single-tape TM (simulate `k` tapes on one with delimiters) — quadratic slowdown.
- **Nondeterministic TM** ≡ deterministic TM (breadth-first search over the computation tree) — exponential slowdown.
- **Enumerator** ≡ Turing-recognizable: a language is recognizable iff some enumerator prints it.
- Robustness of the model is the evidence for the **Church-Turing thesis**: intuitive algorithm = Turing machine.
- The thesis is not a theorem — it is a claim about the informal notion of algorithm.
- Hilbert's 10th problem as the historical motivation.
