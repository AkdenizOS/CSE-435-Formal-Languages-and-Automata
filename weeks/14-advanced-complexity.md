## Goals
- Place PSPACE and the space classes relative to P and NP.
- Know what the course covered and how the pieces fit together.

## Key concepts
- **SPACE(f(n))**, PSPACE, NPSPACE; **Savitch's theorem**: `NSPACE(f) ⊆ SPACE(f²)`, so PSPACE = NPSPACE.
- `P ⊆ NP ⊆ PSPACE ⊆ EXPTIME`; `P ⊊ EXPTIME` by the time hierarchy theorem, so at least one inclusion is strict.
- PSPACE-completeness; TQBF as the canonical PSPACE-complete problem.
- L and NL; NL-completeness.
- The full picture: regular ⊂ CFL ⊂ P ⊆ NP ⊆ PSPACE ⊂ decidable ⊂ recognizable ⊂ all languages.
