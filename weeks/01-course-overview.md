## Goals
- Know what the three areas — automata, computability, complexity — each ask.
- Understand how the course is graded and what the exams look like.

## Key concepts
- The central question: what can be computed, and how efficiently?
- Automata theory → models of computation. Computability theory → what is solvable at all. Complexity theory → what is solvable efficiently.
- The Chomsky hierarchy as a roadmap: regular ⊂ context-free ⊂ decidable ⊂ Turing-recognizable.
